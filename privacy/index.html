Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026.

What SdmApk collects

Visitors do not need an account, and SdmApk does not ask you for personal details. The site does not include advertising or analytics trackers.

Hosting and logs

SdmApk runs on Cloudflare. Like any host, Cloudflare processes technical request data such as your IP address to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Fonts

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page opens.

What is stored in your browser

If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your browser so it can be remembered. The admin area uses a sign-in cookie, which is only set for the site owner.

Download links

App files are hosted on other services. When you open a download link, that service's own privacy policy applies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
